Keratoconus

One of the World's Most Advanced Keratoconus Centres

From molecular diagnostics and advanced imaging to the best lasers for sculpting the cornea, and on to the future of gene therapy, we manage keratoconus across its whole journey: to strengthen, reshape and restore vision.

Led by

Prof Rohit Shetty, one of the world’s leaders in keratoconus management.

Cornea care
  1. Understand

    Molecular diagnostics

    Genetic and tear-biomarker tests to understand why the cornea is weakening, and who is at risk.

  2. Detect

    Advanced imaging

    Tomography, epithelial mapping and biomechanics to detect keratoconus early and track every change.

  3. Reshape

    Laser sculpting

    iVIS Suite and SCHWIND AMARIS lasers sculpt and regularise the corneal shape, customised to each eye.

  4. Restore

    Future therapeutics

    Planning for tomorrow’s treatments, including gene therapy, with our research lab.

Dry Eye

Pillar 08: Dry Eye, Decoded by Biomarkers

Dry eye is an enigma. We approach it holistically: we read the biomarkers in the tears, target treatment to them, and adjust as those biomarkers change. It is among the world's first translational-science approaches to dry eye.

  • Tear biomarker profiling

    Finding the cause, not just treating the symptom.

  • Targeted therapy

    Treatment matched to the biology of each eye.

  • Progress you can measure

    Tracked by how the biomarkers respond.

  • The whole picture

    Systemic, hormonal and lifestyle factors considered together.

“Treat the biology, not just the symptom.”
