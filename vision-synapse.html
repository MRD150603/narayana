Our Unique Specialities

The eye is not an island.

Narayana Netralaya nVision keeps the old charm of an ethical hospital while looking to the future of ophthalmology. We see the eye as a synapse, connected to the brain, sleep, immunity, development and the way we live. The Vision Synapse brings together a family of speciality clinics that you will not find under one roof anywhere else.

Explore the six clinics

  • Mind & cognition

    How the brain interprets, adapts to and remembers what we see.

  • Sleep & rhythm

    Sleep and circadian health as drivers of ocular comfort and nerve health.

  • Immunity & tears

    Inflammation and immune signals read from the tear film outwards.

  • Development

    How a child's growing brain and eyes shape a lifetime of vision.

  • Movement & lifestyle

    Activity, work and environment as part of every eye's story.

Speciality clinics that set us apart

Clinic 01 / 06

X-PLAIN

A centre and a culture for the unexplained

We begin where answers are incomplete. Clinical observations, imaging, biology and the patient's lived experience come together to turn unexplained findings into questions we can study, and into more precise care.

  • Unexplained pain
  • Genetic blindness
  • Non-responders
Clinic 02 / 06

The Sleep and Eye Clinic

Because the eye rests when you do

Poor sleep, sleep apnoea and disrupted body clocks leave their mark on the eyelids, the tear film and the optic nerve. We look at the eye and sleep together, so that the treatment of one supports the other.

  • Sleep apnoea & eyelids
  • Ocular surface
  • Circadian health
Clinic 03 / 06

Cognition, Neuroadaptation & Eye Clinic

We see with the brain as much as the eye

After cataract or refractive surgery, the brain has to learn to see again. We study and support that neuroadaptation, and the wider links between vision, perception and cognition across life.

  • Neuroadaptation
  • Visual perception
  • Eye–brain health

The Immune Eye Clinic

When the body's defences turn towards the eye

Autoimmune and inflammatory disease often shows itself first in the eye. We map the immune signals in tears and tissues and treat them in step with the rest of the body, alongside physician colleagues.

  • Autoimmune eye disease
  • Ocular inflammation
  • Immune profiling
Clinic 05 / 06

CODE

Centre for Occupational Dry Eye

Screens, air-conditioning, long shifts and demanding environments are changing the modern eye. CODE combines workplace and lifestyle assessment with research-led, biomarker-guided dry eye care.

  • Screen & workplace dry eye
  • Lifestyle & ergonomics
  • Dry eye research
Clinic 06 / 06

The Eye Tumour & Oncology Centre

Detect early. Treat precisely. Preserve sight.

From childhood retinoblastoma to tumours of the eye surface and orbit, we combine early detection, precise treatment and long-term care, with the aim of saving life, the eye and sight.

  • Retinoblastoma
  • Ocular surface tumours
  • Orbital & eyelid tumours
